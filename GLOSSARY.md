# Portfolio Site

The personal site of a fullstack engineer: a curated record of projects, skills, and working method, presented in three locales.

## Language

**AI-first development**:
A working method in which the engineer specifies, sets the review bar, and reviews what matters, while AI agents (primarily Claude Code) implement, test, refactor, and review every change against that bar, so one person ships what previously took a small team. A claim about how the work was done, never about what the product does.
_Avoid_: AI-assisted, AI-powered, AI usage, vibe coding

**AI feature**:
An LLM-backed capability shipped inside a product. Not what this site claims about its projects; kept in the glossary only to mark the boundary.
_Avoid_: AI integration, AI usage

**Team AI-first migration**:
The act of moving an engineering team from individual, ad-hoc AI tool use to one shared, standardized AI-first development workflow.
_Avoid_: AI adoption, AI rollout

**Project**:
One entry in the Work section: a real engagement with a name, a stack, dates, a highlight, achievements, and a description, stored as a content file.
_Avoid_: Case study, portfolio item

**Highlight**:
A project's one-line summary shown in the scroller.
_Avoid_: Tagline, subtitle

**Achievement**:
One outcome-focused bullet on a project page, stating what was delivered and its measurable effect.
_Avoid_: Responsibility, task

**Stack**:
A project's flat list of technologies, which also feeds the Hard Skills chart.
_Avoid_: Tech, tools, technologies

**Role line**:
The one-line identity under the name on the home page.
_Avoid_: Title, tagline, headline

**Surface**:
One of the site's content areas: Home, About, Hard Skills, Work, Contact.
_Avoid_: Page, section, screen

**Composition**:
A project page's bespoke arrangement, chosen by the project's slug; a project without one is shown as the generic reading column. Each composition tells one story about its project and owns its own loud element in place of the hero numeral.
_Avoid_: Layout, template, custom page, case-study page
